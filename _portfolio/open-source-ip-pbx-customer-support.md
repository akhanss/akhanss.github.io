Legacy phone-based PBX systems for customer support often lack accountability: operators may be away from their desks, leave calls unattended, or fail to register complaints, with little management visibility into such lapses. As part of his M.Engg. project, developed an Asterisk-based, open-source IP-PBX system to address this, and deployed it for customer support service at Dhaka Power Distribution Company Limited (DPDC). The company continues to provide support services for a large portion of its customers through this channel. See also the related [conference paper](/publication/2015-01-01-asterisk-open-source-ip-pbx).
