Provided academic and technical supervision throughout the design, development, and deployment of the DIU Alumni Association Election Portal, leveraging DIU's faculty and student resources. Contributions included guiding requirement analysis, ensuring secure software engineering practices, validating system architecture, and overseeing the development workflow to maintain accuracy, transparency, and data integrity. Core features delivered on time included voter authentication, secure ballot management, real-time result monitoring, and an administrative control panel, resulting in a reliable and user-centric digital voting platform.
