Designed a low-cost, IoT-based solution to automatically evaluate two standard key performance indicators for the continuity of power supply in a distribution network. On top of the sensing solution, developed a web-based, real-time power interruption monitoring system for further outage analysis, strategic decision-making, and improved customer service.

This work received national ICT awards in 2020 under two different categories: (i) Digital Government, and (ii) Internet of Things. See also the related publications: [conference paper](/publication/2021-01-01-low-cost-iot-power-interruption-monitoring-conf) and [book chapter](/publication/2019-01-01-low-cost-iot-power-interruption-monitoring-chapter).
