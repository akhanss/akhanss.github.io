Developed and deployed an automated image validation process for Dhaka Power Distribution Company Limited (DPDC) using convolutional neural networks, to identify and resolve complaints against meter readers for not visiting customers' premises to properly record energy consumption. The solution uses a state-of-the-art CNN architecture with transfer learning to detect useful validation images, and the technology was handed over to the organization.

The solution was recognized with national ICT awards in 2020 under two distinct categories: (i) Research & Development (R&D), and (ii) Technology (Artificial Intelligence). It is a potential model for image-based automatic meter reading, particularly for developing countries such as Bangladesh.
