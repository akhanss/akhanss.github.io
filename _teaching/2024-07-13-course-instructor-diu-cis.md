Since joining Daffodil International University in July 2024 (as Assistant Professor, later promoted to Associate Professor in the Department of Computing and Information System), Dr. Khan has taught undergraduate and graduate courses including:

* Artificial Intelligence
* Machine Learning
* Deep Learning
* Big Data Analytics
* Data Analytics and Business Modeling
* Structured Programming
* Data Structure
* Algorithm Design & Analysis
* Computer Networks

Beyond the classroom, he mentors students on machine learning, AI problem solving, experimental reasoning, and competition strategy, and contributes to curriculum preparation and compliance with Bangladesh Accreditation Council (BAC) standards.
