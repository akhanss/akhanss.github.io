Dr. Mohammad Azam Khan is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computing and Information System (CIS) at Daffodil International University (DIU), Dhaka, Bangladesh. He also serves as a coach and core academic team member of the Bangladesh Artificial Intelligence Olympiad (BdAIO), contributing to national team selection, training, and mentoring for international AI competitions.

He previously served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Software Engineering (SWE) at DIU. Before joining DIU, he was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence, KAIST, Republic of Korea, and a Research Engineer at EnterTake Co., Ltd., Seoul. Earlier in his career he held several engineering positions at Dhaka Power Distribution Company Limited (DPDC), one of Bangladesh's largest power distribution utilities.

He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Korea University, Seoul, South Korea (supervisor: Prof. Jaegul Choo, Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence, KAIST), and his M.Engg. and B.Sc.Engg. in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) and International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC), respectively.

Current Roles
======
* Associate Professor, Dept. of Computing and Information System (CIS), Daffodil International University (Jan. 2026–present)
* Coach, Team Bangladesh, National AI Olympiad program (BdAIO)

Under his coaching, Team Bangladesh achieved a historic debut at the Asia-Pacific Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (APOAI) 2026, securing 3 of the 10 Gold Medals; the winning team was subsequently invited to meet the Honorable Prime Minister of Bangladesh. He also mentored Team Bangladesh at the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI) 2025 in Beijing, where the team earned two Bronze Medals and one Honorable Mention. More detail on this and his other service activities is on the [Service & AI Olympiad](/service/) page.
