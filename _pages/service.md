AI Olympiad Coaching & Mentoring
======
Dr. Khan serves as a coach and core academic team member of the **Bangladesh Artificial Intelligence Olympiad (BdAIO)**, contributing to national team selection, training, mentoring, and preparation for international AI competitions.

* **Coach, Team Bangladesh — Asia-Pacific Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (APOAI) 2026, China.** Team Bangladesh achieved a historic debut, securing 3 of the 10 Gold Medals. The winning team was invited to meet the Honorable Prime Minister of Bangladesh, Tarique Rahman, in recognition of this success.
* **Mentor and Coach, Bangladesh National AI Team — International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI) 2025, Beijing, China.** The team earned two Bronze Medals and one Honorable Mention.

Beyond the international Olympiad arena, he has contributed as a convener, co-convener, judge, and mentor for several national AI competitions and initiatives:

* Co-Convener, AI Project Competition 2026, Daffodil International University
* Convener, Agentic AI Competition 2025, Daffodil International University
* Judge, National AI Build-a-thon 2026, MillionX Bangladesh
* Judge, The Infinity AI BuildFest 2026, BRAC University
* Judge, IEEE International Congress on AI & Digital Health Innovation (ICADHI 2026), DIU
* Judge, Bangladesh AI Olympiad 2025 (organized by DIPTI)
* Judge, 3MT Competition, Daffodil International University
* Member, Technical Setup, ACM ICPC Asia Dhaka Regional 2024

Mentoring Junior Researchers
======
Dr. Khan has provided guidance and support to junior researchers beyond the scope of his own research projects, advising on the technical direction of several studies and participating in manuscript writing. This mentorship has contributed to the acceptance of co-authored papers at top-tier venues, including CVPR and NeurIPS.

Supervising Industrial Projects
======
During his time at Dhaka Power Distribution Company Limited (DPDC), Dr. Khan led an innovation team generating project ideas for the organization and, after his Ph.D., returned to lead two major initiatives — the [Meter Reading Snapshot Validation system](/portfolio/meter-reading-snapshot-validation-cnn/) and the [Low-Cost IoT-Based Power Interruption Monitoring System](/portfolio/low-cost-iot-power-interruption-monitoring/) — both recognized with National ICT Awards in 2020 across multiple categories.
